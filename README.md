## 🔍 Core Features & Capabilities

### 1. Macro Sentiment & Market Health Bar
- **Bitcoin Dominance (BTC.D)** real-time tracking.
- **Crypto Fear & Greed Index** with historical sentiment grading.
- **30-Day Stablecoin Supply Expansion/Contraction** metrics indicating fiat liquidity inflow velocity.
- **Live Global Price Ticker** with quick-toggle visibility.

### 2. Deep Institutional Due Diligence Reports
For every asset in the catalog or custom-queried token, the terminal compiles:
- **Core Value Proposition & Problem Solved**: Objective technical purpose without marketing hype.
- **Competitive Moat Assessment**: Developer mindshare, network effects, and technological differentiation.
- **Tokenomics & Supply Velocity**: Circulating supply vs. maximum hard cap, staking ratios, validator distributions, and burn schedules.
- **Token Unlock & Dilution Warnings**: Automated alerts on private VC cliff unlocks, foundation allocations, and upcoming supply dumps.
- **On-Chain Vitality Metrics**: 30-day active developer commits, GitHub star growth, active contributor count, daily active addresses (DAA), and daily transaction volume.
- **MVRV Valuation Multiples**: Identifying historical accumulation zones vs. market cycle over-extension.

### 3. Quantitative Charting & Dynamic Key Levels
- High-fidelity price and volume charts across multiple granularities (1H, 24H, 7D, 30D, 1Y).
- Automatic mathematical derivation of:
  - **Macro Resistance & Breakout Targets**
  - **Institutional Support & Value Accumulation Floors**
  - **24-Hour Price Ranges & High/Low Volatility Bands**

### 4. Watchlist & Saved Research Library
- Built-in slide-over drawer to store, organize, and revisit institutional research reports.
- Instant search and filtering across your saved asset repository.
- Quick navigation between the high-level **Market Dashboard** and in-depth **Asset Report** views.
